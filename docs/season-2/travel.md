# Getting to every Season 2 dungeon from Silvermoon

Eight dungeons, three ways in. Read the three rules, then jump to the dungeon you need.

> **Where these numbers come from.** Unlike `abilities.md`, nothing here is read out of the
> game files — no addon on disk stores outdoor entrance coordinates. These are from published
> guides (Method, Icy Veins, Overgear, ConquestCapped), cross-checked against each other on
> **2026-08-19**. Where two sources disagreed I say so inline. Treat a coordinate as
> "within a few yards", not gospel.

## Three rules before you walk anywhere

1. **Check your spellbook first.** Timing a key at **+10 or higher** grants that dungeon's
   teleport, and it lands in the **General** tab of your spellbook — not the Adventure Guide.
   Altar of Fangs' is *Path of Venomous Evolution*. Once you have one, the walk is never a
   problem again.
2. **The three legacy dungeons share one portal.** They are not in Quel'Thalas at all — they
   are behind the **Timeways** portal in Silvermoon (see below). Never fly to Zandalar.
3. **Only one person has to travel.** Whoever gets there first drops a **summoning stone**
   at the entrance; everyone else clicks the party summon. A Warlock does it for free from
   anywhere. On a fresh key this is faster than five people flying.

## The Timeways — Kings' Rest, Temple of Sethraliss, Ruby Life Pools

This is the whole answer for all three legacy dungeons. One portal, three doors.

1. In Silvermoon, go to the **portal room by Wayfarer's Rest** — `53.3, 66.1`. It's the
   same building as the Stormwind/Orgrimmar portals.
2. Look for the **golden portal** with **Lindormi** (the bronze dragon who fixes keystones)
   and **Agmera** standing beside it. If you can see Lindormi, you're in the right spot.
3. Step through. Inside the Timeways is a portal to each Season 2 dungeon that isn't native
   to Midnight — that is, exactly these three.

That's it. You never set foot in Zandalar or the Dragon Isles.

> One guide lists the Timeways at `42.4, 58.3` near the Thalassian University instead. Two
> other sources plus the Lindormi landmark agree on `53.3, 66.1`, so start there.

**Fallback, if you ever want the front door:** Kings' Rest is on **top of the Atal'Dazar
pyramid** in Zuldazar at `37.9, 39.7` — easy to walk into Atal'Dazar by mistake, go up.
Temple of Sethraliss is in the **Slithering Gulch**, northern Vol'dun, `51.9, 26.5`.
Ruby Life Pools is in **southern Waking Shores**, `59.8, 75.9`.

## Murder Row — Silvermoon City

The easiest one in the pool. You are already in the zone.

1. From the portal room, head **east** through the city.
2. The entrance is just outside **Astalor's Sanctum**, at `57.0, 61.0`.

If you're doing a Murder Row key straight after an Altar of Fangs key, note both start
within a few seconds of each other — the Coiled Isle portal is in Astalor's Sanctum too.

## Altar of Fangs — The Coiled Isle

Two hops: portal to the isle, then into the Vaults.

1. **Portal:** in **Astalor's Sanctum** (the Prey hub, east Silvermoon), in the room leading
   up to Wayfarer's Rest — `56.8, 67.4`.
2. It drops you at **Tokka's Landing** on The Coiled Isle. Pick up that flight point.
3. Fly to the **Gate of the Eastern Fang** (~`45.5, 65.0` on the isle map) and go in. This
   puts you in the Vaults of Atal'Utek.
   - Don't take the **Gate of the Serpent's Eye** — that one leads toward the Venomous Abyss
     raid entrance, not the dungeon.
4. Inside the Vaults, head for the **Amani Foothold**. The door is labelled *Altar of Fangs
   Entrance*, at `47.2, 67.6` **on the Vaults map** (the Vaults are their own map, so the
   coordinate won't match the outdoor isle map).

**Prerequisite:** the portal only appears once you've done the short **Prey Season 2 intro
questline**. If it isn't there, that's why.

**Shortcuts once unlocked:** there's a Coiled Isle portal on your **warband housing plot**
(Horde `54.2, 56.1`), and the return portal on the isle is at `58.3, 48.5`.

## The Blinding Vale — Harandar

1. **Portal:** west side of Silvermoon, in the **Gardens of Remembrance**, sitting under a
   tree — `36.9, 68.4`.
2. It drops you at **The Den**, Harandar's hub.
3. Fly **south-west** to `27.0, 78.0`.

**Two things about Harandar that will catch you out:**

- **The zone has no flight masters at all.** There is no "fly to the nearest point" — from
  The Den you fly the whole way yourself, every time. This is the longest travel in the pool
  after you strip out the portals, so it's the best candidate for a summon.
- **The Silvermoon portal requires the campaign done.** Until then the Harandar portal lives
  in **Fairbreeze Village, Eversong Woods**, at `45.3, 47.3`.

## Voidscar Arena — Voidstorm

1. **Portal:** west Silvermoon again, just **above** the Gardens of Remembrance and right
   next to the Harandar one — `35.3, 65.7`. Check which portal you're clicking; they're
   neighbours and go to very different places.
2. Head **north** to **Slayer's Rise**, `53.6, 34.5`. The entrance is inside the PvP area,
   so expect company on a war-mode realm.

**Prerequisite:** Voidstorm opens after the **Into the Abyss** intro chain from **Magister
Umbric** in Silvermoon's Sanctum of Light.

> Coordinates disagree here: one guide says `51.4, 18.5`. Two say ~`53.6, 34.5` and both
> name Slayer's Rise, so go with that; if the door isn't there, sweep north-west.

**Note:** there's no direct Harandar ↔ Voidstorm portal. Both zones' return portals are on
the lower level of The Den in Harandar, and both routes run through Silvermoon.

## Den of Nalorakk — Zul'Aman

The only one in the pool with no portal at any point.

1. Zul'Aman is **south** of Quel'Thalas, past Eversong and the Ghostlands.
2. Take the flight path to the nearest Zul'Aman flight point, or just **fly south** from
   Silvermoon — it's a straight shot down the coast.
3. The entrance is in the far **south** of the zone, at `30.0, 84.5`, inside the **giant
   carved stone bear head** of Nalorakk. You can't miss the landmark. The door is **down the
   stairs** under it.

## If you only remember one thing

Open your spellbook's **General** tab before every key. Every dungeon you've timed at +10 is
already a one-click trip, and the list grows all season.
